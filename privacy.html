Privacy Policy

GOAT IT Publisher · last updated 22 November 2026

GOAT IT Publisher (“the tool”) is a private tool run by one person, the owner of the GOAT IT YouTube channels (“the owner”). Only the owner signs in to it, and only with the owner's own Google account. This policy explains what YouTube data the tool accesses and what it does with that data.

Data the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions, and only for the owner's own channels:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and settings.
  • youtube.readonly: read channel and video details, such as a video's title, status and public view count.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read the channel's analytics reports, such as views, watch time, audience retention and the countries views come from.

The tool does not ask for, and does not read, anyone's email, contacts, comments, messages or payment details.

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's channels and to show the owner how those videos perform. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with other people.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where the data is kept

On the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are kept in a local file that only the owner's computer account can read. Video details and analytics are kept in a local database. Short summaries, such as a video's view count, may be sent to the owner's private notification channel.

How long it is kept and how to delete it

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the tool's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the tool can no longer read or upload anything, and the owner deletes the locally stored tokens and data.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and tracking

These web pages use no cookies, analytics or tracking.
