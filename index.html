GOAT IT Publisher

A private tool that one person, the owner of the GOAT IT YouTube channels, uses to publish and measure their own videos.

What it does

  • Uploads short videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the owner's own YouTube channels: GOAT IT, GOAT IT Podcasts and GOAT IT Tech.
  • Reads those channels' video details and analytics (views, watch time, audience retention and the countries views come from) so the owner can see how each video performs.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it never acts on anyone else's account or channel.
